Our Rotary - Rotaract Family's Focus

Rotary International (2026-2027)

Create Lasting Impact

Together, let's create a lasting impact through selfless service, meaningful action, and unwavering commitment to our communities. Every project we undertake today becomes a legacy of hope for tomorrow.

Rotaract District 3141 Theme

Alchemy

Alchemy reminds us that true transformation begins from within, turning passion into purpose and potential into meaningful action. Ignite the Magic Within is a call to awaken the leader, dreamer, and changemaker in each of us to create a lasting impact through service.

Our Parent Rotary Club

Regal

REGAL symbolizes leadership with grace, service with purpose, and excellence with humility. It reminds every Rotarian to be noble in character, generous in action, and committed to creating a positive impact in society.

Rotaract clubs are part of a global effort to bring peace and international understanding to the world. This effort starts at the community level but knows no limits in its outreach.
